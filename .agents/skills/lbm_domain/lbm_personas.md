# GableLBM LBM Dealer Personas

Use as a reference in Stage 02 when building detailed persona profiles for a specific feature.

---

## Primary Personas

### Outside Salesperson / Account Manager
- **At:** Job sites, in the truck, customer offices
- **Device:** iPhone/iPad primarily; desktop when at the office
- **Core job:** Build relationships, quote jobs, win new business
- **ERP usage:** Quote builder (high volume), order status lookup, customer account history
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Quoting takes too long; can't do it from the field; no visibility into availability or current pricing
- **"Done well" looks like:** Quote to customer in < 15 minutes from a job site

### Inside Sales / Counter Staff
- **At:** The counter in the yard
- **Device:** Desktop (large monitor), sometimes a tablet
- **Core job:** Serve walk-ins and phone orders with speed and accuracy
- **ERP usage:** Quick order entry, inventory lookup, pricing, customer lookup
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Too many clicks for a simple counter sale; slow to look up availability
- **"Done well" looks like:** Customer goes from "I need X" to receipt in under 3 minutes

### Yard Manager / Operations
- **At:** The yard, warehouse, receiving dock
- **Device:** Desktop in yard office, sometimes tablet
- **Core job:** Manage inventory accuracy, coordinate receiving and will-call picking
- **ERP usage:** Inventory adjustments, receiving POs, will-call queue, location management
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Inventory counts are always wrong; receiving is paper-based; no real-time location tracking
- **"Done well" looks like:** Physical count matches the system; received product is available in 10 minutes

### Dispatcher
- **At:** Dispatch office (desktop)
- **Device:** Large desktop monitor (manages many routes simultaneously)
- **Core job:** Plan delivery routes, assign trucks and drivers, handle day-of changes
- **ERP usage:** Dispatch board, route building, driver communication, delivery tracking
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Manual route planning; no real-time visibility into driver status; changes require phone calls
- **"Done well" looks like:** All routes planned by 7am; changes handled without phone calls

### Driver
- **At:** In the truck, on job sites
- **Device:** Android tablet or phone (mounted in cab)
- **Core job:** Make deliveries, collect signatures, report issues (shorts, damage, no access)
- **ERP usage:** Driver app (delivery manifest, navigation, POD capture)
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Paper manifests; manual signature collection; no way to report issues digitally
- **"Done well" looks like:** No paper; every delivery confirmed digitally with photo and signature

### Purchasing / Buyer
- **At:** Office (desktop)
- **Device:** Desktop
- **Core job:** Manage vendor relationships, buy at the right price, maintain service levels
- **ERP usage:** PO creation, vendor performance, reorder points, market indices, commodity pricing
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** No real-time commodity pricing; manual reorder triggers; no vendor scorecards
- **"Done well" looks like:** Reorders happen automatically at the right price; margin is protected

### Controller / AP Clerk
- **At:** Office (desktop)
- **Device:** Desktop
- **Core job:** Financial accuracy — AR, AP, bank reconciliation, period close
- **ERP usage:** Invoice management, payment posting, GL journal entries, bank recon, reporting
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Manual journal entries; bank recon is spreadsheet-based; period close takes days
- **"Done well" looks like:** Period closes in < 4 hours; all transactions auto-post to GL

### Owner / General Manager
- **At:** Office, occasionally remote
- **Device:** Desktop + mobile (review dashboards)
- **Core job:** Drive profitability, manage cash flow, oversee operations
- **ERP usage:** Dashboard, reports, margin analysis, sales performance
- **Pain with legacy ERP:** Can't see real-time margin; reports are end-of-month; no drill-down
- **"Done well" looks like:** Real-time dashboard with margin by product category and salesperson

## Persona Usage Guide

When writing a persona for a specific feature in Stage 02:
1. Start with the base profile above
2. Narrow it to the specific workflow context
3. Define the JTBD specific to this feature (not the general job)
4. Define the specific pain the current ERP causes in this exact workflow
5. Score the opportunity (importance vs. satisfaction today)
